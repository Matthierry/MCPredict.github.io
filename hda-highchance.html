What does “highest chance” mean?

These are fixtures where the model assigns the strongest probability to a specific match result (home win or away win), regardless of price.

  • 📈 Focuses on win probability, not strictly value.
  • 🏠 High-confidence home favourites can appear frequently.
  • 🧳 Strong away sides with favourable match-ups also show here.

You can combine this view with the Best Value page: a selection that appears in both can be a strong candidate, but still never guaranteed.
